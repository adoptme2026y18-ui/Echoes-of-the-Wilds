# Reputation System

## Overview

The Reputation System tracks how individuals, factions, worlds, and the wider game community view a player.

Reputation reflects a player's actions, choices, accomplishments, and relationships.

Players can build trust and respect through positive actions or damage their reputation through negative behavior.

---

## Reputation Types

Echoes of the Wilds uses multiple reputation categories.

### NPC Reputation

Each NPC maintains their own opinion of the player.

NPC reputation reflects:

* Conversations
* Quest completion
* Trades
* Assistance
* Previous interactions
* Other player actions

Different NPCs may view the same player differently.

---

### Faction Reputation

Factions maintain separate reputation values.

Player actions affecting one faction may not affect another.

Faction reputation helps determine access to faction-specific content and opportunities.

---

### World Reputation

Individual worlds maintain their own reputation values.

Actions performed within a world may influence how that world's inhabitants react to the player.

World reputation encourages players to build relationships across multiple worlds.

---

### Global Reputation

Global Reputation represents a player's overall standing throughout the game universe.

This reputation reflects long-term behavior and accomplishments.

Global Reputation may influence interactions with systems that are not tied to a specific NPC, faction, or world.

---

## Reputation Values

Players can view their exact reputation values.

Reputation information is available through dedicated interface systems.

Players always know where they stand with NPCs, factions, worlds, and global reputation.

---

## Positive Reputation

Players may earn reputation through activities such as:

* Completing quests
* Assisting NPCs
* Trading fairly
* Helping communities
* Discovering valuable information
* Supporting faction goals
* Other positive actions

---

## Negative Reputation

Reputation can become negative.

Negative reputation may result from actions such as:

* Betrayal
* Criminal behavior
* Failing obligations
* Damaging relationships
* Other harmful actions

Specific reputation penalties will be defined separately.

---

## Reputation Recovery

Negative reputation is not permanent.

Players may repair damaged relationships over time.

Methods may include:

* Completing quests
* Providing assistance
* Making amends
* Performing helpful actions
* Other reputation-recovery activities

Players always have opportunities to improve their standing.

---

## Reputation Rewards

Building reputation unlocks benefits and opportunities.

Potential rewards include:

### Discounts

Merchants may offer improved prices.

---

### Special Quests

Trusted players may receive unique quest opportunities.

---

### Special Items

Certain items may only become available to respected players.

---

### Crafting Knowledge

NPCs, factions, or worlds may share exclusive recipes and crafting techniques.

---

### Portal Knowledge

Players may gain access to clues regarding portal locations and activation requirements.

---

### Magical Knowledge

Trusted individuals may share clues regarding hidden spells, magical discoveries, and magical schools.

---

### Additional Opportunities

Future systems may introduce additional reputation-based rewards.

---

## NPC Memory Integration

NPC memory directly supports the Reputation System.

NPCs remember previous interactions and may adjust their behavior accordingly.

Building trust requires consistent positive actions over time.

---

## Long-Term Progression

Reputation serves as a long-term progression system.

Players who invest time into building relationships gain access to additional opportunities throughout the world.

---

## Design Goals

The Reputation System should:

* Make player actions meaningful.
* Reward positive relationships.
* Support roleplaying and player choice.
* Encourage community interaction.
* Create long-term goals.
* Strengthen world immersion.

Players should feel that their actions leave a lasting impact on the people and places they encounter.
