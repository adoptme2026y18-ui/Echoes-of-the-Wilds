# Player Marketplace and Contracts

## Overview

The Marketplace and Contract System allows players to create a living, player-driven economy.

Players can buy, sell, trade, commission work, create jobs, and offer rewards for services performed by other players.

The system is intended to support every playstyle, including traders, crafters, explorers, hunters, collectors, tamers, and adventurers.

---

# Marketplace

## Overview

The Marketplace allows players to sell items and assets directly to other players.

It serves as the primary hub for player-to-player commerce.

---

## Listings

Players may create an unlimited number of active marketplace listings.

There is no limit to the number of items a player may place on the marketplace.

---

## Listing Duration

Marketplace listings remain active until sold.

Listings do not expire automatically.

This allows players to list rare or valuable items without worrying about time restrictions.

---

## Sellable Assets

Players may sell nearly anything that can legally be traded within the game.

Examples include:

* Equipment
* Resources
* Crafting materials
* Collectibles
* Consumables
* Decorations
* Mounts
* Companions
* Pets
* Other eligible assets

Specific restrictions will be defined separately if needed.

---

## Marketplace Philosophy

The marketplace is designed to support a healthy player-driven economy.

Players should be able to specialize in:

* Trading
* Crafting
* Collecting
* Resource gathering
* Breeding and taming systems
* Exploration
* Other professions

---

# Contracts

## Overview

Contracts allow players to create jobs and requests for other players.

Contracts create opportunities for cooperation and economic interaction.

---

## Public Contracts

Players may create public contracts.

Any eligible player may accept and complete a public contract.

---

## Private Contracts

Players may create private contracts.

Private contracts are assigned to a specific player.

Only the designated player may complete the contract.

---

## Contract Payment

Contract rewards are paid after successful completion.

The contract creator provides payment once the agreed objective has been fulfilled.

Specific verification and security systems will be defined separately.

---

## Contract Types

Players may create contracts for a wide variety of activities.

Examples include:

### Resource Gathering

Request resources from gatherers.

Examples:

* Mining materials
* Lumber resources
* Fishing supplies
* Other resources

---

### Crafting Contracts

Request crafted items from skilled crafters.

Examples:

* Equipment
* Tools
* Decorations
* Consumables
* Other crafted goods

---

### Delivery Contracts

Request transportation or delivery of items.

---

### Hunting Contracts

Request the defeat of specific creatures or completion of hunting objectives.

---

### Collection Contracts

Request rare collectibles, resources, or discoveries.

---

### Exploration Contracts

Request players to locate specific places, objects, portals, or discoveries.

---

## Custom Contracts

Players may create additional contract objectives as supported by future systems.

The contract system is intended to remain flexible and expandable.

---

# Bounty System

## Overview

Players may create bounties.

Bounties are specialized contracts that offer rewards for completing difficult or valuable objectives.

---

## Bounty Examples

Examples may include:

* Hunting dangerous creatures
* Obtaining rare resources
* Finding valuable collectibles
* Completing difficult tasks
* Special exploration objectives

Specific bounty categories will be expanded throughout development.

---

# Economic Integration

The Marketplace and Contract System are closely connected.

Together they create:

* Economic opportunities
* Community interaction
* Player-driven progression
* Alternative playstyles
* Long-term goals

Players should be able to earn wealth through many different paths.

---

# Design Goals

The Marketplace and Contract System should:

* Encourage player interaction.
* Support the economy.
* Reward specialization.
* Create meaningful jobs.
* Promote cooperation.
* Enable player-created opportunities.

Players should feel that the world continues to evolve through the actions of its community.
