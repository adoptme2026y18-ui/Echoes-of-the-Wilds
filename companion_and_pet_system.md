# Companion and Pet System

## Overview

Echoes of the Wilds features both Companions and Pets.

Although both are collectible creatures, they serve different purposes and fulfill different roles within gameplay.

Companions actively travel alongside players and assist them during their adventures.

Pets remain at the player's base and contribute through trust, collection, and base-related interactions.

---

# Companions

## Overview

Companions are active partners that accompany players throughout the world.

Companions provide benefits, buffs, and support during gameplay.

Players are encouraged to build teams of companions that complement their preferred playstyles.

---

## Active Companion Limit

Players may have:

* 1 to 3 active companions at a time

The exact number available may depend on progression systems defined elsewhere.

---

## Companion Progression

### Companion Levels

Companions gain experience through adventures and activities.

As companions level up, they become stronger and more effective.

---

### Companion Growth

Leveling companions may improve:

* Support effectiveness
* Buff strength
* Utility abilities
* Other companion-related attributes

Specific progression details will be defined separately.

---

## Companion Benefits

Companions provide positive benefits to players.

Examples may include:

* Stat boosts
* Utility bonuses
* Resource bonuses
* Exploration bonuses
* Combat support
* Other beneficial effects

Specific companion abilities are determined individually.

---

## Companion Collection

Players are encouraged to collect and develop multiple companions.

Different companions may be better suited for different activities and situations.

---

# Pets

## Overview

Pets are collectible creatures that remain at the player's base.

Unlike companions, pets do not travel with the player during adventures.

Pets provide a sense of life, personality, and progression within player bases.

---

## Active Pet Limit

Players may have:

* Up to 10 active pets at their base at one time

Additional pets may be stored through systems defined elsewhere.

---

## Pet Progression

Pets do not gain traditional levels.

Instead, pets use a Trust system.

---

## Trust System

Trust represents the bond between a player and a pet.

Trust increases through interaction and care.

As trust increases, additional benefits, interactions, or rewards may become available.

Specific trust mechanics will be defined separately.

---

## Pet Purpose

Pets are intended to:

* Personalize player bases
* Reward collecting
* Create emotional attachment
* Provide long-term goals
* Enhance the living world feeling

Pets are companions of the home rather than companions of adventure.

---

# Collection Philosophy

Both companions and pets are intended to reward:

* Exploration
* Discovery
* Collection
* Long-term progression

Players should feel encouraged to build meaningful relationships with the creatures they encounter throughout the world.

---

# Design Goals

The Companion and Pet System should:

* Encourage collecting.
* Reward long-term investment.
* Support multiple playstyles.
* Add personality to player experiences.
* Create memorable bonds with creatures.

Companions should enhance adventures.

Pets should make a player's home feel alive.
