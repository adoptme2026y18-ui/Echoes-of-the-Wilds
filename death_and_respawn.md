# Death and Respawn System

## Overview

The Death and Respawn System is designed to minimize frustration while preserving meaningful consequences in specific situations.

Most deaths are intended to be learning experiences rather than severe punishments.

Players are encouraged to take risks, explore, and experiment without fear of losing long-term progress.

---

## Standard Death

When a player dies during normal gameplay:

* No items are lost.
* No equipment is lost.
* No Gold is lost.
* No experience is lost.
* No skills are reduced.

Death does not remove player progression.

---

## Respawn

After death, players may choose where to respawn.

Available respawn locations depend on the locations the player has previously unlocked.

---

## Respawn Locations

Players may select from eligible respawn points they have discovered.

Examples may include:

* Home bases
* Special respawn locations
* Other unlocked respawn points

Specific respawn point types will be defined separately.

---

## Death Penalties

There are no standard death penalties.

Players do not receive:

* Stat reductions
* Gold penalties
* Experience penalties
* Equipment damage
* Other punishment systems

The goal is to encourage exploration and experimentation.

---

## Player Revives

Before respawning, a defeated player may be revived by another player.

Successful revival returns the player to active gameplay without requiring respawn.

Specific revival mechanics will be defined separately.

---

## Companion Behavior

When a player dies:

* Active companions return to the player's base.

Companions remain safe and are not permanently harmed by player death.

---

## Mount Behavior

When a player dies:

* Active mounts return to the player's base.

Mounts remain safe and can be summoned again later.

---

# PvP Death Rules

## Overview

PvP deaths follow different rules than standard deaths.

PvP is intended to involve meaningful risk and reward.

---

## Item Drops

When a player dies in a PvP zone:

* The player's items are dropped.

Dropped items may potentially be collected by other players.

Specific item-drop rules will be defined separately.

---

## PvP Risk

The possibility of losing items creates meaningful stakes within PvP areas.

Players enter PvP zones knowing the risks involved.

---

# Special Respawn Locations

## Unlockable Respawn Points

Players may discover and unlock additional respawn locations throughout the world.

Unlocking new respawn points improves travel flexibility and exploration opportunities.

---

## Exploration Rewards

Discovering respawn locations serves as another reward for exploration.

Players who travel widely gain additional convenience and strategic options.

---

# Design Philosophy

The Death and Respawn System is designed around:

* Player freedom
* Reduced frustration
* Exploration encouragement
* Meaningful PvP risk
* Cooperative gameplay

Death should be a temporary setback rather than a severe punishment.

---

# Design Goals

The Death and Respawn System should:

* Encourage exploration.
* Reduce unnecessary frustration.
* Support cooperative play through revives.
* Create meaningful PvP consequences.
* Preserve long-term progression.
* Reward world discovery.

Players should feel comfortable taking risks while still respecting the dangers of PvP zones.
