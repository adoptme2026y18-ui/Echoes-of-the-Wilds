# Echoes of the Wilds

> Every journey leaves an echo.

Echoes of the Wilds is a player-driven online fantasy RPG where players forge their own path through a vast living world filled with adventure, discovery, and danger.

Explore untamed wilderness, uncover ancient secrets, tame powerful creatures, and build your legacy in a world shaped by exploration and progression.

## Features

- Open-world fantasy adventure
- Player-driven progression system
- Mounts for travel and exploration
- Collectible and trainable pets
- Recruitable companions
- Diverse monsters and creatures
- Exploration-focused gameplay
- Quests, events, and hidden discoveries
- Crafting and resource gathering
- Multiplayer online experience

## Gameplay

Players are free to choose how they progress. Become an explorer, monster hunter, collector, merchant, craftsman, legendary adventurer, etc. Your choices determine your journey.

Travel across unique regions, discover rare creatures, collect powerful equipment, and uncover the mysteries hidden throughout the wilds.
