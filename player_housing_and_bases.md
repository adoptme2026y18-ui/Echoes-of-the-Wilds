# Player Housing and Bases

## Overview

Player Bases serve as personal homes, storage centers, pet habitats, crafting locations, and long-term progression hubs.

Bases allow players to express creativity, build communities, and establish a permanent presence within the world.

Bases are designed to grow alongside the player throughout their journey.

---

## Base Plots

Players may only build on designated base plots.

Plots are specifically designed for player housing and construction.

This system helps maintain world organization while still providing significant building freedom.

---

## Base Ownership

Players may own an unlimited number of bases.

There is no limit to the number of plots a player can acquire and develop.

Players are free to create specialized bases across multiple worlds.

Examples include:

* Main homes
* Crafting bases
* Farming bases
* Pet sanctuaries
* Trading hubs
* Collection museums

---

## Multi-World Housing

Bases can exist in every world.

Players are free to establish homes wherever they choose.

Different worlds may support different building styles, layouts, and opportunities.

---

## Base Persistence

Bases remain permanently.

A player's base is never removed due to inactivity.

Players can return after long breaks and find their homes exactly as they left them.

---

## Visitors

Players may visit other players' bases.

Base visiting encourages:

* Community interaction
* Creativity sharing
* Trading
* Social activities
* Exploration

---

## Cooperative Building

Players may allow others to help build their bases.

However, cooperative construction requires a Helper Stand.

---

## Helper Stand

The Helper Stand grants building permissions to other players.

Without a Helper Stand, only the base owner may modify the base.

The owner controls who receives building access.

This system protects player creations while supporting collaboration.

---

## Pets

Pets reside at player bases.

Players may have up to:

* 10 Active Pets per Base

Pets help make bases feel alive and personalized.

Pets do not travel with players during adventures.

---

## Crafting Facilities

Bases can contain crafting facilities.

Examples include:

* Crafting stations
* Workshops
* Processing areas
* Specialized crafting structures

Specific facilities will be defined separately.

---

## Farming

Bases may support farming activities.

Players can develop agricultural areas and other resource-producing sections of their property.

Specific farming systems will be defined separately.

---

## Storage

Bases may provide storage-related benefits.

Storage systems allow players to organize resources, collectibles, equipment, and other valuables.

---

## Companion Support

Bases may contain features that support companion growth and management.

Specific companion-related facilities will be defined separately.

---

## Personal Expression

Housing is intended to be a major source of player creativity.

Players should be encouraged to customize:

* Layouts
* Decorations
* Structures
* Themes
* Displays
* Collections

Every base should feel unique to its owner.

---

## Design Goals

The Housing System should:

* Encourage creativity.
* Support long-term progression.
* Reward collecting.
* Promote community interaction.
* Provide meaningful utility.
* Create a lasting sense of ownership.

A player's base should feel like their home within the world of Echoes of the Wilds.
