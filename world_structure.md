# World Structure

## Overview

Echoes of the Wilds is built around a multi-world structure.

Players can explore multiple worlds, each offering unique experiences, challenges, opportunities, resources, and progression paths.

The world structure is designed to encourage exploration, discovery, and long-term adventure.

---

## Multiple Worlds

The game contains multiple worlds.

Each world may contain its own:

* Regions
* Activities
* Resources
* Creatures
* Events
* Currencies
* Discoveries

Worlds are intended to feel distinct while remaining connected through the larger game universe.

---

## World Travel

Players can travel between worlds using portals.

Portals serve as the primary method of inter-world transportation.

Travel between worlds is unlocked through exploration and progression.

---

## Portal Discovery

Portals must be discovered or constructed.

Players unlock portals by locating a portal statue and offering required items.

Once the required offerings have been made, the associated portal becomes available.

This system encourages exploration and rewards discovery.

---

## Portal Statues

Portal statues act as gateways between worlds.

Each statue may require different offerings depending on the destination.

Specific offering requirements are defined separately for each portal.

---

## Starter Regions

New players begin their adventure within designated starter regions.

Starter regions provide a safe environment for learning core gameplay systems and beginning character progression.

Specific starter regions will be defined separately.

---

## World Progression

Additional worlds are unlocked through portal discovery and activation.

Players are encouraged to explore and uncover new destinations through gameplay.

---

## World-Specific Currencies

Certain worlds may contain their own unique currencies.

These currencies exist alongside Gold.

World-specific currencies are intended to support unique rewards, activities, progression systems, and economies within their respective worlds.

Specific world currencies will be defined separately.

---

## Area Unlocking

The method for unlocking new areas within worlds has not yet been finalized.

This system will be defined during future development.

---

## Exploration Philosophy

Exploration is a core pillar of the game.

Players should feel rewarded for:

* Discovering new worlds
* Activating portals
* Finding hidden locations
* Uncovering secrets
* Pursuing personal goals

The world structure is designed to support player-driven adventures rather than a single required path.

---

## Design Goals

The world structure should:

* Encourage exploration.
* Reward discovery.
* Support player freedom.
* Create meaningful progression.
* Provide long-term goals.
* Make every world feel worth exploring.

Players should feel that there is always another adventure waiting beyond the next portal.
