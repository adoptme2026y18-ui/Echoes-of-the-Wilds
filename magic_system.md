# Magic System

## Overview

Magic is a powerful progression path within Echoes of the Wilds.

Unlike traditional RPGs, magic is not immediately available to every player.

Instead, players must discover and unlock magical knowledge through exploration, investigation, experimentation, and discovery.

Magic is intended to feel mysterious, rewarding, and deeply connected to the world.

---

## Unlocking Magic

Magic is unlocked later during a player's journey.

All players can eventually learn magic.

No class restrictions prevent players from becoming magic users.

Players remain free to combine magic with any other playstyle.

---

## Discovering Magic

Spells are not automatically learned.

Players must discover hidden clues throughout the world.

These clues may lead players toward unlocking magical knowledge.

Examples include:

* Ancient writings
* Hidden locations
* Mysterious objects
* World discoveries
* Secret encounters
* Other exploration-based opportunities

The exact methods vary between spells.

---

## Magic Skill

Magic is a trainable skill.

Players improve their Magic Skill through use and magical activities.

As Magic Skill increases, additional magical opportunities become available.

---

## Mana

Magic consumes Mana.

Mana is required for:

* Spellcasting
* Magical abilities
* Special magical actions
* Other magic-related systems

Mana management is an important part of magical progression.

---

## Magic Schools

Magic is organized into Magic Schools.

Each school represents a category of magical knowledge.

Players may study multiple schools.

No player is restricted to a single school of magic.

Specific schools will be defined separately.

---

## Spell Learning

Players unlock spells by successfully discovering and deciphering the requirements associated with each spell.

Learning a spell should feel like uncovering a secret rather than simply purchasing an ability.

---

## Spell Creation

Players may create their own spells.

Spell creation encourages experimentation and creativity.

Players can combine magical knowledge to develop unique magical abilities.

Specific spell creation mechanics will be defined separately.

---

## Magical Experimentation

Experimentation is a core part of the Magic System.

Players are encouraged to:

* Test theories
* Explore hidden locations
* Combine magical concepts
* Search for rare magical knowledge

Discovery should be one of the most rewarding aspects of becoming a magic user.

---

## Combat Magic

Magic may be used during combat.

Combat spells may provide:

* Damage
* Support
* Utility
* Defense
* Other combat-related effects

Specific spell categories will be defined separately.

---

## Non-Combat Magic

Magic can also be used outside of combat.

Examples may include:

* Travel
* Exploration
* Resource gathering
* Crafting assistance
* Environmental interactions
* Utility functions

Magic is intended to be useful throughout all areas of gameplay.

---

## Progression Philosophy

Magic is designed to reward:

* Exploration
* Curiosity
* Discovery
* Experimentation
* Long-term dedication

Players who actively search for magical knowledge should continually uncover new opportunities.

---

## Integration with Other Systems

Magic interacts with many game systems, including:

* Combat
* Crafting
* Exploration
* Taming
* Equipment
* Quests
* World progression

Magic should feel woven into the entire game world rather than isolated to combat.

---

## Design Goals

The Magic System should:

* Encourage discovery.
* Reward curiosity.
* Support creativity.
* Promote experimentation.
* Provide meaningful progression.
* Create memorable magical moments.

Magic should feel like uncovering the hidden secrets of the world rather than simply unlocking abilities from a menu.
