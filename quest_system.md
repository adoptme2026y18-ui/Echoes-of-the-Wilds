# Quest System

## Overview

The Quest System is designed to provide goals, rewards, stories, and opportunities without forcing players onto a predetermined path.

Players may pursue quests as often as they choose, but quests are not required for progression.

Quests exist to support player adventures rather than define them.

---

## Player Freedom

Quests are completely optional.

Players can progress through:

* Exploration
* Combat
* Crafting
* Gathering
* Taming
* Trading
* Collecting
* Other gameplay activities

A player can ignore quests entirely and still experience full character progression.

---

## Quest Types

The game supports multiple quest types.

### Handcrafted Quests

Handcrafted quests are individually designed experiences.

These quests may provide:

* Unique rewards
* Lore
* Special encounters
* World discoveries
* Achievement opportunities

---

### Procedural Quests

Procedural quests are generated dynamically by game systems.

These quests help provide endless gameplay opportunities.

Examples may include:

* Gathering objectives
* Hunting requests
* Delivery tasks
* Exploration goals
* Crafting requests

Specific procedural quest generation rules will be defined separately.

---

## Daily Quests

Daily Quests refresh every day.

Daily Quests provide:

* Additional rewards
* Consistent objectives
* Repeatable content
* Progression opportunities

Players are free to participate or ignore them.

---

## Guild Quests

Guild Quests are cooperative objectives designed for guild members.

Guild Quests encourage teamwork and community participation.

Examples may include:

* Resource gathering
* Hunting challenges
* Exploration projects
* Crafting initiatives
* Community events

Specific guild quest content will be defined separately.

---

## Player-Created Quests

Players may create quests for other players.

This system allows the community to generate its own opportunities and objectives.

Examples may include:

* Resource requests
* Crafting contracts
* Hunting bounties
* Collection requests
* Delivery jobs

Player-created quests support a player-driven economy and community interaction.

---

## Quest Scaling

Quest difficulty and rewards may scale based on player progression.

Scaling helps keep content relevant for both new and experienced players.

Specific scaling formulas will be defined separately.

---

## Quest Rewards

Quest rewards may include:

* Experience
* Gold
* Equipment
* Resources
* Collectibles
* Reputation
* Special currencies
* Other rewards

Reward structures depend on quest type and difficulty.

---

## Quest Discovery

Quests may be obtained through:

* NPCs
* Guild systems
* Exploration
* Events
* Player-created contracts
* World discoveries
* Other game systems

Players are encouraged to discover opportunities naturally through gameplay.

---

## Long-Term Goals

The Quest System should provide an endless source of optional content.

Players should always have opportunities to pursue new adventures regardless of their preferred playstyle.

---

## Design Goals

The Quest System should:

* Support player freedom.
* Encourage exploration.
* Reward creativity.
* Promote community interaction.
* Provide optional goals.
* Create memorable adventures.

Quests should enhance a player's story rather than replace it.
