# Portal System

## Overview

The Portal System serves as the primary method of travel between worlds in Echoes of the Wilds.

Portals are not immediately available to players and must be discovered through exploration.

The system is designed to encourage discovery, cooperation, and long-term progression.

---

## Portal Discovery

Portal statues are hidden throughout the game world.

Players must locate these statues through exploration.

Portal statues cannot be crafted or placed by players.

They exist as permanent world structures.

---

## Portal Activation

A discovered portal statue is initially inactive.

To activate a portal, players must provide the required offerings.

Offerings vary depending on the portal and destination world.

Once all requirements have been fulfilled, the portal becomes active.

---

## World Access

Activated portals allow travel between worlds.

Each portal connects to a specific destination.

Players may freely travel through portals they have unlocked.

---

## Individual Progression

Portal progression is tracked individually.

Each player must unlock portals for their own character.

Discovering and activating a portal is a personal achievement.

---

## Community Assistance

Although portal progression is tracked individually, players may assist one another in gathering the required offerings.

This encourages cooperation and community interaction.

---

## Shared Portal Knowledge

Players are free to share information regarding portal locations and activation requirements.

The game encourages exploration but does not restrict community collaboration.

---

## Permanent Unlocks

Once a portal has been activated, it remains unlocked permanently.

Activated portals cannot become deactivated.

Players never lose access to portals they have unlocked.

---

## Exploration Rewards

The Portal System is intended to reward:

* Exploration
* Discovery
* Curiosity
* Cooperation
* Long-term progression

Finding a new portal should feel like a meaningful achievement.

---

## Future Expansion

Additional portals, worlds, and activation requirements may be introduced through future updates.

The Portal System is designed to support ongoing world expansion.

---

## Design Goals

The Portal System should:

* Encourage exploration.
* Reward discovery.
* Support player-driven adventures.
* Promote cooperation between players.
* Create memorable milestones.
* Provide meaningful access to new content.

Every portal should represent the beginning of a new adventure.
