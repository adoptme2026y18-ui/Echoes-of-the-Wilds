# Inventory and Storage System

## Overview

The Inventory and Storage System is designed to provide players with convenient item management while supporting long-term progression, collecting, trading, and exploration.

The system prioritizes accessibility and player freedom while minimizing unnecessary inventory restrictions.

---

## Player Inventory

Each player begins with:

* 40 Inventory Slots

Inventory slots can hold any valid item.

Items stack infinitely.

This allows players to focus on gameplay rather than constantly managing stack sizes.

---

## Equipment Storage

Equipment is managed separately through the Equipment System.

Characters have 20 dedicated equipment slots that do not consume inventory space while equipped.

---

## Bank System

Players have access to a personal bank.

The bank can be accessed from anywhere through the Bank Access button located within the player's inventory interface.

The bank provides long-term storage for items, equipment, resources, collectibles, and other valuables.

---

## Starting Bank Capacity

Players begin with:

* 100 Bank Slots

---

## Bank Expansion

Bank capacity can be expanded indefinitely.

Each upgrade provides:

* +100 Bank Slots

Cost:

* 10 Gold per upgrade

There is no maximum bank size.

Players may continue expanding their storage as needed.

---

## Item Storage

The bank supports:

* Equipment
* Resources
* Crafting materials
* Collectibles
* Consumables
* Other eligible items

Items stored in the bank remain available until withdrawn by the player.

---

## Trading

Players can trade items directly with other players.

Trading allows players to exchange:

* Equipment
* Resources
* Crafting materials
* Collectibles
* Other eligible items

Trading supports player interaction and a player-driven economy.

---

## Marketplace

Echoes of the Wilds includes a player marketplace.

The marketplace allows players to:

* List items for sale
* Purchase items from other players
* Participate in the game's economy

The marketplace is intended to support collectors, crafters, traders, adventurers, and other playstyles.

---

## NPC Vendors

Players may sell items to NPC vendors.

NPC vendors provide an additional method of converting unwanted items into gold.

---

## Item Dropping

Players may drop items onto the ground.

Dropped items can be used for:

* Sharing items
* Inventory management
* Community events
* Other gameplay interactions

Specific rules regarding dropped item ownership and pickup permissions will be defined separately.

---

## Design Goals

The Inventory and Storage System should:

* Reduce inventory frustration.
* Support collecting and hoarding playstyles.
* Encourage player trading.
* Support a healthy economy.
* Remain easy to use.
* Reward long-term progression.

Players should spend more time adventuring and less time fighting inventory limitations.
