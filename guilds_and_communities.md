# Guilds and Communities

## Overview

Guilds allow players to form organized communities, work together toward shared goals, and create lasting social connections.

Guilds are intended to support cooperation, exploration, trading, events, quests, and community-driven gameplay.

Players can choose to adventure alone or join a guild to participate in larger group activities.

---

## Guild Creation

Players may create their own guilds.

Guild founders become the initial leaders of their guild and are responsible for managing guild settings, permissions, and membership.

Additional guild management roles may be defined separately.

---

## Guild Size

Each guild may contain up to:

* 20 Members

The guild size limit is intended to encourage active communities while maintaining a close-knit group atmosphere.

---

## Guild Bases

Guilds may own and develop Guild Bases.

Guild Bases serve as shared community spaces where members can gather, collaborate, and participate in guild activities.

Guild Bases are separate from personal player bases.

---

## Guild Storage

Guilds have access to Guild Storage.

Guild Storage allows members to organize and share resources within the guild.

Each guild member receives:

* 50 Personal Guild Storage Slots

Items placed within a member's personal guild storage remain associated with that member.

Additional shared storage systems may be defined separately.

---

## Guild Quests

Guilds may participate in Guild Quests.

Guild Quests provide goals designed for cooperative play.

Examples may include:

* Exploration objectives
* Resource collection goals
* Hunting objectives
* Crafting projects
* Community challenges

Specific Guild Quest content will be defined separately.

---

## Guild Cooperation

Guilds are designed to encourage teamwork.

Members can work together to:

* Complete quests
* Gather resources
* Build guild facilities
* Organize events
* Explore worlds
* Assist with progression goals

---

## Guild Communication

Guilds provide players with a dedicated community structure for organizing activities and maintaining social connections.

Additional communication features will be defined separately.

---

## Guild Portals

Guilds do not possess shared portals.

Portal progression remains tied to individual players.

Each player must discover and unlock portals on their own character.

Guild members may still assist one another in gathering portal activation requirements.

---

## Community Building

Guilds are intended to strengthen the social aspects of the game.

Players should feel encouraged to:

* Help one another
* Share knowledge
* Organize adventures
* Create events
* Build lasting communities

---

## Design Goals

The Guild System should:

* Encourage cooperation.
* Support community growth.
* Reward teamwork.
* Create meaningful social experiences.
* Provide shared progression opportunities.
* Strengthen player connections.

Guilds should feel like communities built by players rather than simple membership lists.
