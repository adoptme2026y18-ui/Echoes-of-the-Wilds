# Combat Design

## Overview

Combat in Echoes of the Wilds is designed to be flexible, engaging, and accessible to different playstyles.

The game uses a hybrid combat system that combines action-oriented gameplay with ability-based combat mechanics.

Players should feel in control of their character while still having access to strategic abilities and customization options.

---

## Combat Style

Echoes of the Wilds uses a hybrid combat system.

Combat combines:

* Direct player control
* Ability usage
* Dodging
* Blocking
* Parrying
* Movement-based combat

The goal is to provide both action and strategy without restricting players to a single combat approach.

---

## Player Perspective

Players may choose their preferred perspective.

Available perspectives include:

* First-person
* Third-person

Players can switch between supported perspectives according to their preferences.

---

## Combat Mechanics

### Dodging

Players can dodge incoming attacks.

Successful dodges can help avoid damage and create tactical opportunities during combat.

---

### Blocking

Players can block incoming attacks to reduce or prevent damage.

Blocking rewards awareness and timing.

---

### Parrying

Players can parry attacks.

Successful parries reward skillful play and can create combat advantages.

---

## Resources

### Mana

Mana is used to perform magical abilities and other mana-based actions.

Players must manage mana carefully during extended encounters.

---

### Stamina

Stamina is consumed by physical actions.

Examples may include:

* Dodging
* Sprinting
* Blocking
* Special attacks

Stamina management is an important part of combat strategy.

---

## Mount Combat

Some mounts are capable of participating in combat.

Combat-capable mounts may provide additional offensive or defensive options.

Not all mounts are designed for combat.

---

## Companion and Pet Combat

Some companions and pets can participate in combat alongside players.

Others may provide utility, support, collection, exploration, or other benefits.

The role of each companion or pet depends on its design and purpose.

---

## Player Customization

Players may customize certain combat-related settings.

Examples may include:

* Camera preferences
* Targeting preferences
* Control layouts
* Combat assistance options

These settings allow players to tailor the combat experience to their preferences while maintaining consistent game mechanics.

---

## PvP

Player versus Player combat is limited to PvP Zones.

PvP Zones appear randomly throughout the world.

These locations are not permanently fixed and may change over time.

Players who discover PvP Zones can choose whether or not to participate.

The system encourages exploration, discovery, and dynamic player interactions.

---

## Design Goals

Combat should:

* Reward skillful play.
* Support multiple playstyles.
* Encourage strategic decision-making.
* Feel responsive and satisfying.
* Allow player customization.
* Create memorable encounters.

Combat should be accessible to new players while providing depth for experienced players.
