# Equipment System

## Overview

Equipment plays a major role in character progression within Echoes of the Wilds.

Players can obtain equipment through exploration, combat, crafting, trading, quests, and other activities.

Equipment allows players to customize their builds, improve their capabilities, and prepare for different adventures.

---

## Equipment Slots

Characters have 20 equipment slots.

### Armor Slots

* Helmet
* Chestplate
* Leggings/Pants
* Boots
* Shoulder
* Knees

### Weapon and Tool Slots

* Main Tool/Weapon
* Off-Hand

### Utility Slots

* Back

  * Examples: wings, flight equipment, and other back-mounted gear
* Potions
* Bait

### Spare Equipment Belt

Players have five quick-access spare equipment slots.

* Spare Slot 1
* Spare Slot 2
* Spare Slot 3
* Spare Slot 4
* Spare Slot 5

These slots allow players to quickly switch between tools, weapons, or other equipment.

### Accessory Slots

* Ring
* Necklace
* Charm

### Mount Slot

* Mount

The mount slot allows players to equip their active mount.

---

## Inventory

Players have 40 inventory slots.

Items can stack infinitely.

The inventory system is designed to reduce unnecessary inventory management and allow players to focus on exploration and adventure.

---

## Equipment Acquisition

Equipment can be obtained through multiple methods.

Examples include:

* Crafting
* Exploration
* Loot
* Trading
* Quests
* Special events
* Discoveries

Players are free to pursue their preferred methods of obtaining gear.

---

## Equipment Rarities

Equipment is divided into rarity tiers.

### Common

The most frequently found equipment.

---

### Uncommon

Slightly rarer equipment with improved value.

---

### Rare

Uncommon equipment with stronger bonuses and greater value.

---

### Ultra-Rare

Highly sought-after equipment with powerful benefits.

---

### Legendary

Exceptionally rare equipment known throughout the world.

---

### Ultra Legendary

The rarest equipment in the game.

Ultra Legendary items are intended to be extraordinarily difficult to obtain.

Example target rarity:

* Approximately 0.0001% chance from appropriate sources.

These items are designed to represent major achievements and long-term goals.

---

## Durability

Equipment does not have durability.

Items do not degrade, break, or require repairs.

Players are encouraged to focus on gameplay, progression, and exploration rather than maintenance.

---

## Build Customization

Equipment supports a wide variety of playstyles.

Players can mix and match equipment to create builds that match their goals and preferences.

The equipment system is intended to complement the game's player-driven progression philosophy.

---

## Design Goals

The equipment system should:

* Encourage exploration.
* Reward progression.
* Support multiple playstyles.
* Reduce unnecessary inventory frustration.
* Provide meaningful long-term goals.
* Make rare discoveries exciting and memorable.

Equipment should feel valuable, rewarding, and worth pursuing throughout a player's journey.
