# Monster System

## Overview

Monsters are one of the primary forms of life found throughout Echoes of the Wilds.

They serve as sources of combat, resources, taming opportunities, exploration, quests, and progression.

Monsters are intended to feel like living parts of the world rather than simple enemies waiting to be defeated.

---

## Monster Dispositions

Not all creatures behave the same way.

Monsters fall into three primary behavior categories.

### Hostile

Hostile creatures will attack players when certain conditions are met.

These creatures often present combat challenges and hunting opportunities.

---

### Neutral

Neutral creatures generally ignore players unless provoked.

They may defend themselves if attacked or threatened.

---

### Friendly

Friendly creatures do not attack players.

These creatures may provide taming opportunities, interactions, or other benefits.

---

## Monster Rarities

Monsters possess rarity tiers.

Rarity influences factors such as:

* Discovery frequency
* Rewards
* Taming difficulty
* Resource quality
* Collection value

Specific rarity names and balancing will be defined separately.

---

## World Movement

Monsters are capable of moving throughout the world.

Creatures are not permanently tied to a single location.

Migration allows the world to feel more alive and dynamic.

Players may discover creatures in unexpected locations as populations move over time.

---

## Creature Behaviors

Creatures may possess unique behavioral traits.

Examples include:

* Aggressive
* Territorial
* Nocturnal
* Pack Hunter
* Defensive
* Curious
* Fast Learner
* Other specialized behaviors

Different creatures may combine multiple traits.

---

## Creature Interactions

Monsters can interact with one another.

Creatures may:

* Compete
* Hunt
* Defend territory
* Travel together
* Fight other creatures

The world continues to function even when players are not directly involved.

---

## Mini Bosses

Certain creatures are classified as Mini Bosses.

Mini Bosses provide greater challenges than normal creatures while remaining accessible to most players.

Mini Bosses are intended to create memorable encounters without becoming overwhelming.

---

## Taming Mini Bosses

Mini Bosses can be tamed.

Successfully taming a Mini Boss is intended to be a significant achievement.

Specific taming requirements are determined individually.

---

## Defeating Monsters

Upon defeat, monsters do not immediately provide rewards.

Players must harvest resources from defeated creatures.

---

## Harvesting

Harvesting is required to collect resources from defeated monsters.

Potential harvest rewards may include:

* Materials
* Resources
* Crafting ingredients
* Rare components
* Special items
* Other creature-related rewards

Specific harvesting systems will be defined separately.

---

## Legendary Summons

Legendary creatures do not naturally appear in the world.

Instead, they must be summoned.

---

## Sacrificial Statues

Legendary encounters are activated through Sacrificial Statues.

Players must provide required offerings to summon a Legendary Creature.

Different statues may require different sacrifices.

---

## Legendary Encounters

Legendary encounters are intended to be major community events.

Players are encouraged to:

* Prepare resources
* Work together
* Explore requirements
* Discover summoning methods

Summoning a Legendary Creature should feel like a major accomplishment.

---

## Hunting Integration

The Monster System is closely connected to:

* Hunting
* Taming
* Crafting
* Quests
* Companions
* Mounts
* Exploration

Players may choose to interact with creatures through many different gameplay styles.

---

## Design Goals

The Monster System should:

* Make the world feel alive.
* Reward exploration.
* Encourage observation.
* Support multiple playstyles.
* Create memorable encounters.
* Promote discovery.

Creatures should feel like living inhabitants of the world rather than simple combat targets.
