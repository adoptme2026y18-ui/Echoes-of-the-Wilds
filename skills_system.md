# Skills System

## Overview

Skills represent a character's knowledge, experience, and mastery of specific activities within the world.

Skills improve through use rather than through character level requirements alone.

Players develop their abilities naturally by participating in the activities they enjoy.

Every skill contributes to a player's unique journey and progression.

---

## Skill Progression

Skills increase through repeated use.

The more a player participates in a skill-related activity, the more experienced they become in that skill.

Examples include:

* Mining improves through mining.
* Lumberjack improves through chopping trees.
* Fishing improves through fishing.
* Cooking improves through preparing food.
* Brewing improves through creating beverages.
* Crafting improves through creating items.
* Magic improves through using magical abilities.
* Taming improves through interacting with and taming creatures.
* Hunting improves through tracking and defeating creatures.

Players grow through action rather than simply reaching a character level.

---

## Character Experience

Skill progression contributes to overall character progression.

As players improve their skills, they also gain experience that contributes toward character levels.

This ensures that many different playstyles can support character growth.

---

## Skill Levels

Skills do not have a maximum level.

Players may continue developing their skills indefinitely.

Long-term progression provides ongoing goals for dedicated players.

---

## Skill Requirements

Certain activities, items, abilities, and content may require minimum skill levels.

Examples include:

* Resources
* Equipment
* Crafting recipes
* Taming opportunities
* Magical abilities
* Special activities

Requirements provide meaningful progression goals and rewards for dedicated training.

---

## Skill Rewards

Increasing a skill can provide multiple benefits.

These benefits may include:

* Improved efficiency
* Higher success rates
* Increased resource yields
* Access to new content
* New abilities
* New recipes
* New interactions
* Enhanced effectiveness

Skills should feel rewarding at every stage of progression.

---

## Skill Freedom

Players are not restricted to a single specialization.

A character can eventually learn and develop every skill in the game.

Players are encouraged to pursue the skills that match their interests while always having the freedom to expand into new areas.

---

---

## Design Goals

The skills system should:

* Reward active participation.
* Encourage player freedom.
* Support multiple playstyles.
* Provide meaningful long-term progression.
* Offer clear goals and rewards.
* Allow players to create their own stories through the activities they choose to pursue.

Skills should be a reflection of a player's journey throughout the world.
