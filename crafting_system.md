# Crafting System

## Overview

Crafting is a major progression path within Echoes of the Wilds.

Players can create equipment, tools, consumables, mounts, decorations, resources, and other valuable items through the crafting system.

Crafting is intended to support exploration, discovery, creativity, trading, and long-term progression.

---

## Crafting Skill

Crafting is a trainable skill.

The Crafting Skill improves through active use.

As players gain Crafting Skill levels, they gain access to additional opportunities, recipes, and crafting possibilities.

Crafting contributes to overall character progression.

---

## Crafting Stations

Crafting requires the use of Crafting Stations.

Different stations may support different crafting categories.

Examples include:

* Equipment crafting
* Tool crafting
* Resource processing
* Mount crafting
* Decoration crafting
* Specialized crafting

Specific crafting stations will be defined separately.

---

## Recipe Discovery

Many recipes must be discovered.

Players may discover recipes through:

* Exploration
* Quests
* Loot
* Events
* Experiments
* World discoveries
* Other gameplay activities

Discovery is intended to be an important part of the crafting experience.

---

## Recipe Knowledge

Not all recipes are immediately known.

Players are encouraged to search the world for new crafting knowledge and hidden crafting opportunities.

Recipe discovery should feel rewarding and meaningful.

---

## Recipe Invention

Players may invent new recipes.

Recipe invention allows experimentation with resources and materials.

Successful discoveries may create entirely new crafting opportunities.

Specific invention mechanics will be defined separately.

---

## Crafting Experimentation

Experimentation is encouraged.

Players should feel rewarded for trying unusual combinations and exploring new possibilities.

The crafting system is intended to support creativity rather than simply following predetermined recipes.

---

## Item Quality

Crafted items may vary in quality.

Higher crafting skill levels may improve crafting outcomes.

Specific quality mechanics will be defined separately.

---

## Rarity Progression

Crafted items can become higher rarity.

Crafting is capable of producing valuable equipment and items across multiple rarity tiers.

Rarity progression may depend on:

* Materials
* Skill level
* Recipe quality
* Crafting methods
* Special resources
* Other factors

Specific rarity mechanics will be defined separately.

---

## Crafting Specialization

Players may pursue specialized crafting paths.

Specialization allows crafters to focus on areas that match their interests and goals.

Examples may include:

* Equipment crafting
* Resource processing
* Mount creation
* Decorative crafting
* Other crafting disciplines

Specific specializations will be defined separately.

---

## Economy Integration

Crafting plays an important role within the player economy.

Crafted items may be:

* Used personally
* Traded directly
* Sold through the marketplace
* Used in player-created contracts
* Used in guild projects

Crafting should remain a viable path to wealth and progression.

---

## World Integration

Different worlds may contain unique crafting materials and crafting opportunities.

Exploration is intended to expand a player's crafting capabilities.

Travel and discovery should provide access to new recipes, resources, and inventions.

---

## Design Goals

The Crafting System should:

* Reward creativity.
* Encourage experimentation.
* Support exploration.
* Create meaningful progression.
* Strengthen the player economy.
* Provide long-term goals.

Crafting should feel like a journey of discovery rather than a simple recipe checklist.
