# Currency and Economy

## Overview

The economy of Echoes of the Wilds is designed to support player freedom, trading, exploration, crafting, collecting, and long-term progression.

Gold serves as the primary currency throughout most of the game world.

Additional currencies may exist within specific worlds, events, seasons, or special content.

---

## Primary Currency

### Gold

Gold is the standard currency used throughout the game.

Players use Gold for:

* Purchasing items
* Trading with players
* Buying from NPC vendors
* Expanding bank storage
* Marketplace transactions
* Other gameplay systems

Gold is intended to be the foundation of the game's economy.

---

## Earning Gold

Players can earn Gold through a variety of activities.

Examples include:

* Selling items
* Completing quests
* Hunting creatures
* Crafting and selling goods
* Trading with other players
* Discoveries and exploration
* Other gameplay activities

Players are not required to use a single method for earning wealth.

---

## Player Trading

Players may trade Gold directly with other players.

Trading supports:

* Item exchanges
* Gold exchanges
* Negotiated deals
* Community-driven commerce

The economy should encourage interaction between players.

---

## Marketplace

Echoes of the Wilds includes a player marketplace.

The marketplace allows players to:

* List items for sale
* Purchase items from other players
* Participate in the player economy

There are no marketplace listing fees.

There are no marketplace sales taxes.

Players receive the full value of their successful sales.

---

## NPC Vendors

NPC vendors buy and sell items.

Players may sell unwanted items to vendors in exchange for Gold.

NPC vendors provide an alternative to player trading.

---

## Special Currencies

Certain worlds, events, seasonal activities, or special content may introduce unique currencies.

These currencies exist separately from Gold.

Examples may include:

* Event currencies
* Seasonal currencies
* World-specific currencies
* Special activity currencies

Special currencies are designed to support unique rewards and progression systems.

---

## Currency Limits

There is no maximum amount of Gold a player may possess.

Players may continue accumulating wealth indefinitely.

---

## Debt

Players cannot possess negative Gold.

The economy does not support debt systems.

Transactions cannot reduce a player's Gold below zero.

---

## Economic Philosophy

The economy should:

* Support all playstyles.
* Encourage player interaction.
* Reward effort and dedication.
* Allow multiple paths to wealth.
* Support collectors, crafters, traders, hunters, and explorers.
* Remain accessible to new players while providing long-term goals for experienced players.

No single activity should be the only viable source of wealth.

---

## Design Goals

The economy should feel:

* Fair
* Rewarding
* Player-driven
* Flexible
* Accessible
* Long-lasting

Players should have the freedom to build their wealth through the activities they enjoy most.
