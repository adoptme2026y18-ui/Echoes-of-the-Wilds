# Mount System

## Overview

Mounts are an important part of exploration, progression, travel, and adventure in Echoes of the Wilds.

Players can collect, train, equip, and level a wide variety of mounts throughout their journey.

Different mounts may specialize in different forms of travel and gameplay.

Mounts are intended to become long-term companions rather than temporary tools.

---

## Obtaining Mounts

Mounts can be acquired through multiple methods.

### Taming

Certain mounts can be obtained through taming.

Players must discover and successfully tame eligible creatures to add them to their collection.

---

### Purchasing

Some mounts can be purchased through vendors, merchants, or other game systems.

---

### Quest Rewards

Certain mounts may be awarded through quests, achievements, events, or special accomplishments.

---

### Crafting

Some mounts may be created through crafting systems.

Crafted mounts require specific resources and recipes.

---

## Mount Ownership

Players may own an unlimited number of mounts.

There is no maximum mount collection size.

Players are encouraged to collect and train as many mounts as they wish.

---

## Mount Summoning

Players may summon their mounts from anywhere unless restricted by specific gameplay situations.

Mount access is intended to remain convenient and accessible.

---

## Mount Progression

### Mount Levels

Mounts gain experience and levels over time.

Leveling allows mounts to become stronger and more capable companions.

---

### Mount Skills

Mounts may learn and improve skills.

Different mounts may possess different abilities and progression paths.

Specific mount abilities are defined separately.

---

## Mount Equipment

Mounts can equip gear.

Mount equipment may provide:

* Increased effectiveness
* Utility benefits
* Travel improvements
* Combat advantages
* Other bonuses

Specific mount equipment types will be defined separately.

---

## Mount Categories

Mounts may belong to different travel categories.

### Ground Mounts

Specialized for land travel.

---

### Flying Mounts

Capable of aerial travel.

---

### Water Mounts

Specialized for water-based exploration and travel.

---

### Climbing Mounts

Capable of traversing difficult terrain and vertical surfaces.

---

## Combat Mounts

Some mounts are capable of participating in combat.

Combat mounts may assist players during encounters.

Not all mounts are designed for combat.

---

## Mount Defeat

Mounts do not die permanently.

If a mount is defeated, it becomes unavailable until revived.

---

## Revival Fragments

Defeated mounts can be revived using a Revival Fragment.

Players must possess a Revival Fragment in order to restore a defeated mount.

Mount revival is intended to add value and responsibility without permanently removing player progress.

---

## Collection Philosophy

Mounts are intended to be:

* Valuable companions
* Long-term progression goals
* Meaningful collectibles
* Important travel tools

Players should feel rewarded for expanding and developing their mount collection.

---

## Design Goals

The Mount System should:

* Encourage exploration.
* Reward collection.
* Support multiple playstyles.
* Provide meaningful progression.
* Make travel enjoyable.
* Create emotional attachment to mounts.

Mounts should feel like trusted companions that grow alongside the player throughout their journey.
