# Character Progression

## Overview

Character progression in Echoes of the Wilds is designed to reward a variety of playstyles and activities.

Players are free to pursue their own goals and develop their character through the activities they enjoy most.

There is no maximum character level.

Players can continue progressing indefinitely as they explore the world and expand their skills.

---

## Character Levels

Characters gain experience points (XP) by participating in activities throughout the game.

Different activities provide different amounts of experience.

Examples may include:

* Combat
* Exploration
* Gathering
* Crafting
* Taming
* Questing
* Discoveries

The amount of experience awarded should reflect the effort, risk, and value of the activity.

---

## Level Progression

There is no level cap.

Players may continue earning levels and developing their character over time.

Long-term progression is intended to provide ongoing goals for dedicated players.

---

## Stat Points

When a character gains a level, they receive stat points.

Players may distribute these points according to their preferred playstyle.

Stat choices should allow players to customize their character and create unique builds.

---

## Stat Availability

Available stats may depend on progression paths, archetypes, classes, or other progression systems unlocked by the player.

The exact implementation of this system will be defined separately.

The goal is to provide meaningful character customization while maintaining flexibility.

---

## Respecialization

Players may reset and redistribute their stat points.

Respecialization costs:

* 5 Gold

This allows players to experiment with different builds while still attaching value to their choices.

---

## Design Goals

Character progression should:

* Reward many different activities.
* Support player freedom.
* Encourage experimentation.
* Provide long-term goals.
* Allow players to evolve their playstyle over time.

Progression should feel personal and reflect the unique journey of each player.
