# Taming System

## Overview

The Taming System allows players to befriend, capture, train, and care for creatures throughout the world.

Taming is a major progression path and connects directly to the Mount, Companion, and Pet systems.

Players who invest time into taming can build unique collections and create meaningful bonds with creatures they discover.

---

## Taming Eligibility

Every creature in the game can be tamed.

Different creatures may require different levels of effort, skill, resources, and preparation.

Some creatures are easier to tame, while others may represent long-term goals for experienced tamers.

---

## Taming Skill

Taming is a trainable skill.

Players improve their Taming Skill through successful taming activities and related gameplay.

Higher Taming Skill unlocks access to more difficult taming opportunities.

---

## Taming Requirements

Many creatures require a minimum Taming Skill level before they can be successfully tamed.

More powerful, rare, or challenging creatures generally require greater taming expertise.

---

## Taming Process

Taming involves multiple elements.

These may include:

* Feeding creatures
* Building trust
* Using taming-related items
* Participating in taming activities
* Other creature-specific requirements

Different creatures may respond differently to taming attempts.

---

## Taming Failure

Taming attempts can fail.

Failure is part of the taming process and encourages preparation and skill development.

---

## Failed Taming Attempts

When a taming attempt fails, the creature runs away.

Players may need to locate the creature again before attempting another taming attempt.

Specific recovery mechanics will be defined separately.

---

## Creature Roles

A tamed creature may serve different purposes depending on the creature's design.

Possible roles include:

### Pets

Pets remain at the player's base.

They do not accompany players during adventures.

Pets use the Trust System instead of levels.

---

### Companions

Companions travel with players.

They provide buffs, bonuses, and support.

Companions can level up and become stronger over time.

---

### Mounts

Mounts provide transportation and exploration benefits.

Some mounts may also participate in combat.

Mounts can level up and learn abilities.

---

## Multiple Roles

Some creatures may qualify for multiple roles.

Depending on the creature, a player may choose whether the creature becomes:

* A Pet
* A Companion
* A Mount

Specific creature role options will be defined separately.

---

## Creature Breeding

Players may breed tamed creatures.

Breeding allows players to expand their collections and pursue new creature combinations.

Specific breeding mechanics will be defined separately.

---

## Rare Variants

Rare creature variants may exist.

Variants may include:

* Unique appearances
* Different colors
* Special traits
* Distinct bonuses
* Other rare characteristics

Rare variants are intended to provide exciting collection goals.

---

## Exploration and Discovery

Taming encourages exploration.

Players are rewarded for:

* Finding new creatures
* Discovering rare variants
* Improving Taming Skill
* Expanding collections
* Experimenting with different taming strategies

Exploration should remain a core part of the taming experience.

---

## Collection Philosophy

The Taming System is intended to support:

* Collectors
* Adventurers
* Explorers
* Breeders
* Mount enthusiasts
* Companion-focused players

Players should always have new taming goals to pursue.

---

## Design Goals

The Taming System should:

* Reward patience and dedication.
* Encourage exploration.
* Support long-term progression.
* Create meaningful creature bonds.
* Offer exciting collection opportunities.
* Connect naturally with other game systems.

Taming should feel like a journey of discovery rather than a simple collection mechanic.
