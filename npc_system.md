# NPC System

## Overview

Non-Player Characters (NPCs) are living inhabitants of the world of Echoes of the Wilds.

NPCs provide services, knowledge, interactions, employment opportunities, world-building, and progression support.

NPCs are intended to feel like individuals with their own routines, preferences, and memories rather than static game objects.

---

## Dynamic NPCs

NPCs are capable of moving throughout the world.

They are not permanently fixed to a single location.

NPCs may travel, relocate, visit different areas, and participate in world activities.

This movement helps create a more living and believable world.

---

## NPC Interactions

Players can interact with NPCs in a variety of ways.

Examples include:

* Conversations
* Trading
* Learning information
* Receiving guidance
* Hiring workers
* Building relationships
* Other interaction systems

---

## Relationships

Players may build relationships with NPCs.

Over time, friendships and trust can develop through repeated interaction.

Different NPCs may react differently based on their experiences with the player.

---

## NPC Memory

NPCs remember player actions.

Examples may include:

* Previous conversations
* Assistance provided
* Quests completed
* Trades performed
* Positive interactions
* Negative interactions

NPC memories help make relationships feel meaningful and persistent.

---

## NPC Personalities

Each NPC may possess unique preferences, behaviors, and personality traits.

These differences help create a diverse and believable population.

Specific NPC personalities are defined individually.

---

## Quest Participation

NPCs may provide quests.

NPCs can provide both unique quests and repeatable quests.

Repeatable quests allow players to continue assisting NPCs, earning rewards, building relationships, and participating in ongoing activities.

Different NPCs may offer different repeatable opportunities depending on their profession, location, and relationship with the player.

NPCs do provide traditional repeatable quest chains.

Daily quests are handled through separate game systems.

---

## Knowledge and Discovery

NPCs may provide clues regarding:

* Magic discoveries
* Hidden locations
* Portal activation requirements
* Crafting knowledge
* World secrets
* Other forms of progression

NPCs are intended to contribute to exploration and discovery.

---

## Trading and Shops

Many NPCs may operate shops or provide services.

Examples include:

* Vendors
* Merchants
* Specialists
* Trainers
* Service providers

Specific NPC services are defined separately.

---

## Base Workers

Players may hire NPC workers for their bases.

Workers can assist with various base-related activities.

Examples may include:

* Crafting support
* Farming support
* Resource processing
* Maintenance tasks
* Other specialized jobs

Specific worker systems will be defined separately.

---

## World Integration

NPCs are part of the living world.

They may:

* Travel
* Socialize
* Work
* Explore
* Participate in events

NPC behavior should contribute to the feeling that the world exists beyond the player's actions.

---

## Community Impact

NPCs may react differently to players depending on reputation, relationships, and previous actions.

Player choices can influence future interactions.

---

## Design Goals

The NPC System should:

* Make the world feel alive.
* Encourage relationship building.
* Reward exploration and conversation.
* Support progression systems.
* Create memorable characters.
* Increase immersion.

NPCs should feel like genuine inhabitants of the world rather than simple sources of rewards.
